No robust standalone ML advantage established

The fixed nonlinear model improves on a negative linear baseline, not on the strongest simple factor. Its standalone IC confidence interval includes zero, its mean does not exceed every shuffled-label control, and missing-outcome completion bounds do not establish positive full-cohort association. No trading promotion or reserved-year evaluation is authorized by these results.

The prespecified paired comparison, HGB7 minus Ridge100 = 0.01067, has a 95% interval [0.00238, 0.01833]. That relative development improvement is positive under the stated calendar-block estimator; it does not establish positive standalone predictive alpha or nonlinear superiority over reversal. All intervals use 1,000 circular 20-session calendar-block resamples, preserving the six dates without observed IC. Three label-shuffle controls are diagnostics, not a high-powered randomization test.

Design and scale

The source population contains 11,220 US equity-security IDs, including non-common-stock instruments. Eligibility uses only past/current prices and liquidity: minimum $5 price, $10 million trailing 20-session average dollar volume, complete required history, and a 2,500-security daily cap. There are 1,530,082 decision rows across 2007–2010 and 820,028 forecast rows per specification in 2009–2010.

Twelve fixed price/volume features feed two Ridge and two shallow histogram-gradient-boosting specifications. Three within-training-date label-shuffled HGB controls produce 14 total fits across two annual folds. Training targets mature strictly before each forecast year; target ranks precede deterministic sampling, and dates receive equal weight. Features are observed through close t, with an assumed entry close t+1 and label exit close t+6. Both forecast years are development, not final confirmation.

Missing outcomes are not deleted

Of 820,028 forecast rows per specification, 808,828 have observed labels. The remaining rows comprise 126 missing entries, 551 missing exits, 54 internal gaps and 10,469 fixed right-boundary rows. The 731 non-boundary missing outcomes remain in coverage denominators and score-decile diagnostics; they are not imputed as flat returns. Observed-case Spearman IC is conditional on label availability.

HGB7's mean non-boundary full-cohort Kendall tau-a completion interval is [−0.00115, 0.00241]. These are conservative, tie-aware missing-outcome bounds, not a Spearman confidence interval. They assume latent outcomes exist and do not reconstruct terminal payouts.

Limits that prevent stronger claims

  • Historical security-type/exchange membership and source revision timestamps are not qualified. Latest metadata and auto-close dates are not eligibility inputs.
  • Split/dividend mechanics are checked causally, but the label is a vendor-adjusted price-return proxy, not exact cash-distribution total return or qualified terminal wealth.
  • Missing delisting payouts and merger consideration remain unresolved. The panel is not claimed to be survivor-complete ordinary common stocks.
  • The learning sample is old, short, and spans unusual regimes. It does not establish current-market effectiveness.
  • No executable portfolio, transaction-cost/borrow model, capacity estimate, net PnL or Sharpe is computed here.
  • 2011 received a prior coverage audit but no prediction-performance evaluation. It remains reserved, not described as never accessed.

Audit trail and next decision

The native verifier checks 32 frozen artifacts, six source snapshots, eight bound cache files, 14 fit/forecast lifecycles and local registry bindings without deserializing models. This establishes internal consistency, not scientific validity or external immutability.

The next research question is whether a separately registered model adds information beyond reversal and remains stable across regimes—not whether more tuning can improve this same score. No new fits or holdout release were performed for this review.
