Does the frozen ML forecast add information beyond simple factors?

Post-hoc exploratory analysis of 2009–2010 development. Original V1 results were already observed. No new model was fitted; 2011 performance remains disabled. Pointwise intervals do not correct selection or multiplicity.

Overall measurements

Paired HGB7 minus reversal5 daily IC: -0.02564, 95% 20-session calendar-block interval [-0.05577, 0.00346].

HGB7 partial rank correlation after linear projection of both score and outcome ranks on reversal5 and momentum21: -0.00407, interval [-0.03093, 0.02092]. This is an observed-case descriptive statistic, not a tradable residual signal or general conditional-independence test.

Why this is not deployment evidence

  • Post-hoc development diagnostics; all intervals are pointwise and not selection/multiplicity adjusted
  • Partial rank correlation is descriptive linear projection on the common observed cohort, not a tradable forecast
  • Cross-sectional groups are defined before labels and are not aggregate market regimes
  • Missing-outcome Kendall bounds from V1 do not transfer to partial correlations
  • All V1 data-vintage, historical classification, corporate-action and terminal-wealth limitations remain

No combination weights, signs, features, subgroups or hyperparameters were searched. Outcome projection uses same-date observed returns for evaluation only; no coefficients or per-security residuals are exported. Missing outcomes remain in forecast denominators, and undefined partial correlations remain undefined. There is no PnL or trading-promotion gate.
