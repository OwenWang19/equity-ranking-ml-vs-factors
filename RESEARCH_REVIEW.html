2. The causality contract

Close t: past-only features and dynamic eligibility
  → close t+1: assumed entry reference
  → close t+6: five-session vendor-adjusted price-return proxy
  → training allowed only after that label has matured
  • The source population contains 11,220 security IDs, not 11,220 verified common stocks. Daily eligibility uses a $5 price floor, $10m trailing average dollar volume, required history and a 2,500-security cap.
  • Twelve fixed price/volume features are cross-section ranked before future-label availability is checked. Latest security-type, symbol lifetime and auto-close metadata do not define eligibility.
  • Annual forecasts in 2009 and 2010 use strictly earlier, matured training outcomes. Full observed-cohort target ranks precede deterministic sampling; each training date receives equal weight.
  • All 2011 raw sessions are excluded before this study's feature/label calculation. The earlier 2011 coverage audit is disclosed, so it is not described as never-accessed data.

Inspect causal features/labels, maturity purging and sampling, the fixed model protocol, and hand-worked temporal/corporate-action tests.

3. What prevents an optimistic backtest

  • Missing outcomes are not silently deleted. Per specification, 731 non-boundary missing outcomes and 10,469 fixed-boundary rows remain in forecast denominators. Observed-case IC and conservative Kendall completion bounds are distinct estimands.
  • ML is compared with useful baselines. The stronger reversal baseline, all three frozen shuffled-label controls, both forecast years, all five declared cross-sectional conditions and all fixed 10/20/40-session block sensitivities are retained.
  • No favorable subgroup is promoted. HGB7 partial correlation is negative in 2009 and positive in 2010. A shuffled-label control also has a positive pointwise partial interval, underscoring why isolated significance is not a discovery.
  • Partial correlation is evaluation, not a new strategy. SVD projects both observed score ranks and outcome ranks on the two control ranks plus an intercept. Same-date outcome projection is descriptive; no residual portfolio, coefficients or per-security residuals are exported.
  • Trials remain visible. The 14 model fits are frozen. One diagnostic attempt failed before summary statistics due to pandas datetime-unit equality; one explicitly logged same-protocol compatibility retry completed. Both attempts remain in the audit trail; no model refit occurred.

Inspect the partial-rank implementation, independent projection/degeneracy tests, cohort, date-unit and recovery tests, and the append-only local registry snapshot.

The defensible conclusion is not “ML never works.” It is that this fixed price/volume setup has not demonstrated a stable, useful ML increment over the simple alternatives in this dataset. Rejecting that setup with an inspectable audit trail is a valid research outcome.
